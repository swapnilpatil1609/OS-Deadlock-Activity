Deadlock prevention

Explore how operating systems prevent deadlocks by breaking at least one of the four necessary conditions.

⛨ PREVENTION STRATEGIES
ⓘ
Core idea

Deadlock prevention designs the system so at least one necessary deadlock condition can never hold. This differs from Banker's Algorithm, which avoids unsafe allocations by checking the system state before granting requests.
